Capability system

Eight modules built around the same operating context.

ARA Global eCRM
CRM

CRM core

Leads/customers, branches, contacts, activities, ownership, reassignment, and CSV import.

DAY

My Day

Daily sales planning, voice notes, transcripts, suggested actions, insights, and end-of-day review.

PIPE

Pipeline

Configurable opportunity stages, owner splits, target tracking, and follow-up driven views.

PROP

Proposals

Commercial summaries, GST line totals, statuses, and external document or Canva links.

ORD

Orders

Accepted-proposal conversion with PO metadata and preserved commercial snapshots.

PROD

Production

Order-line work items, templates, stage updates, notes, owners, and due dates.

FIN

Finance

Invoice, payment, cost, gross-margin, incentive, split, and approval records.

RPT

Reports

Read-only views for billings, pipeline, collections, production, clients, and products.

Design principle: ownership supports responsibility, filtering, targets, and incentives; it does not hide company records from other Sales users.
