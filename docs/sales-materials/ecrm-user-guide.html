Getting started

01

eCRM is a single-company CRM. Admin and Sales users see company-wide operating data. Ownership is used for responsibility, filtering, targets, and incentives—not to hide records from other Sales users.

Local development/demo access only: Use the Admin or Sales account assigned for the current environment. Credentials are environment-specific and are not published in this guide.

CRM foundation

Manage leads and customer context

04

Leads and customers are company-level account records with branches, contacts, activities, notes, owner history, and reassignment.

Add one lead
  1. Open Leads.
  2. Choose Add one lead.
  3. Enter account details, state, owner, source, industry, and notes.
  4. Save the record.
Import a CSV
  1. Choose Import leads from CSV.
  2. Follow the fixed template.
  3. Upload and review row-level errors.
  4. Import valid rows.

Pursuit · commercial control

Opportunities and proposals

05

Opportunities track separate pursuits under a lead/customer. Proposals belong to opportunities and hold the structured commercial record.

Create the proposal
  1. Open an opportunity.
  2. Create a proposal from its proposal area.
  3. Add commercial summary, assumptions, inclusions, exclusions, payment terms, and timeline.
Control the record
  1. Add catalog-backed line items.
  2. Attach external PDF or optional Canva links.
  3. Move through sent, accepted, rejected, expired, or withdrawn states.

Book · hand off · deliver

Orders and production

06

Orders are created from accepted proposals and preserve customer, owner, line items, GST totals, PO metadata, and source-proposal context.

Book the order
  1. Open the accepted proposal.
  2. Choose Book order or open the existing order link.
  3. Enter PO metadata and due dates.
  4. Verify the commercial snapshot.
Run production
  1. Open Production.
  2. Open stage controls or a work-item detail.
  3. Update status, owner, due date, notes, completion, or skipped reason.
  4. Scan progress by customer and order.

Roles, rules, and current boundaries

08

Use this page as the operating contract for the current MVP.

Role permissions

Admin

  • All commercial and operational routes
  • Catalog and production configuration
  • Finance records and incentive controls
  • Company-wide reporting

Sales

  • Company-wide CRM and pipeline context
  • My Day planning and voice notes
  • Opportunities, proposals, and orders

Current product boundaries

01
Single-company workspace

This build is not a browser-selectable multi-tenant CRM.

02
External proposal documents

eCRM links document metadata and optional Canva designs; it does not replace a document editor.

03
Finance workflow, not accounting sync

Invoice, payment, cost, margin, and incentive records are supported; invoice PDF and accounting integrations are outside scope.

04
Web application

Native mobile apps, Microsoft/Google login, and email sync are outside the current MVP.

05
Two application roles

Finance and Operations are workflow modules—not separate user roles.

The operating promise

Every handoff stays visible: who owns it, what was agreed, what is due, what has been delivered, and what has been collected.

Cleaner execution

Daily work, calls, notes, follow-ups, and account context stay connected.

Controlled handoffs

Accepted commercial terms carry into orders before delivery work begins.

Visible economics

Billings, collections, costs, margin, incentives, and progress share one view.
